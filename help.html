Getting Started
First-time setup in 5 steps
1️⃣
Complete the Onboarding Wizard
On first launch POSH walks you through naming your shop, choosing a theme, and setting your daily sales goal. This only runs once.
2️⃣
Add Your Products
Go to Stock tab → tap + (top-right). Enter name, selling price, cost price, stock qty, and category. You can also scan a barcode.
3️⃣
Set Your PINs
Default Admin PIN is 4321, Staff PIN is 1234. Change both via Sidebar (☰) → Change Admin PIN / Change Staff PIN.
4️⃣
Set a Daily Sales Goal
Home tab → tap the goal ring or Settings → Daily Goal. POSH fires confetti 🎉 when you hit it.
5️⃣
Install as an App (PWA)
Android: tap the Install App banner or Chrome → Add to Home Screen. iPhone: Safari Share → Add to Home Screen. Works fully offline once installed.

App Modules
What every tab and section does
🏠
Home — Dashboard
Daily summary: revenue, profit, transaction count, goal progress, low-stock alerts, and recent sales with 7-day sparklines.
🛒
POS — Point of Sale
Main selling screen. Tap products, choose payment method (Cash, M-Pesa, Card, Credit), and confirm. Stock updates automatically.
📦
Stock — Inventory
Add, edit, and manage all products. Restock, set low-stock alerts, and scan barcodes. Admin only.
📊
Reports
Revenue, gross profit, expenses, and net profit for Today / Week / Month / All Time with charts and exports. Admin only.
🧾
History
Every completed sale with full item breakdown, payment method, profit, and timestamp. Filter by date. Admin only.
📒
Credit Book
Track credit customers. See who owes what, mark payments, send WhatsApp reminders, and log partial payments.
👤
CRM — Customers
Customer directory with purchase history, total spend, and outstanding credit for building repeat-customer relationships.
⚙️
Settings
Shop name, goal, currency, theme, idle timer, storage mode, and staff accounts. Admin only.

Making a Sale — Step by Step
From product tap to confirmed receipt
①
Open the POS Tab
Tap POS in the bottom nav. You'll see your product grid with stock badges.
②
Tap Products to Add to Cart
Each tap adds 1 unit. The cart FAB shows the running total. Tap it to adjust quantities or remove items.
③
Tap Checkout
Opens the receipt preview. Enter the amount tendered to auto-calculate change.
④
Choose Payment Method
Cash — physical money, enter amount for change calculator.
M-Pesa — mobile money.
Card — debit/credit card.
Credit — auto-adds to Credit Book.
⑤
Confirm Sale
Tap Confirm Sale. Stock is deducted, the sale is recorded, and a cha-ching sound plays 🎵. Cart clears automatically.
💡Pro tip: Star (⭐) your most-sold products to pin them as Favourites at the top of the POS screen for faster checkout.

Stock & Inventory
Adding products, restocking, and product types
Go to Stock → tap the + button (top-right).
Fill in: Name · Category · Selling Price · Cost Price · Stock Qty · Unit · Low Stock Alert · Supplier (optional)
Select the Product Type:
  • Standard — regular packaged items
  • Bulk / Loose — sold by weight or volume
  • Service — no stock tracking (airtime, M-Pesa, printing)
Tap Save.
In the Stock tab, find the product and tap the +📦 restock button. Enter the quantity received and optionally the cost paid. Tap Add Stock.
Each product has a Low Stock Alert threshold (default: 5). When stock drops to or below this number the product card shows an amber badge and the Dashboard shows a warning.
💡 Set it to your typical reorder point — e.g. if you reorder when you have 10 left, set the alert to 10.

Roles & PINs
Admin vs Staff — who can do what

Owner/manager role. Default PIN: 4321. Has access to everything including sales data, expenses, settings, and staff management.

Staff — POS Mode
Cashier/sales rep role. Default PIN: 1234. Can sell, view credit book, and use CRM. Cannot access financials or settings.

If you also forgot the recovery number, contact support at ojiambodavid598@gmail.com with your Shop Code.

Cloud Sync & Data
Backup, restore, offline mode, and multi-device
🔒
Local Mode (Default)
All data stays on this device in the browser's localStorage. Fast, private, works 100% offline. Data is lost if you clear browser storage or switch browsers.
☁️
Cloud Mode
Enable in Settings → Storage Mode. Data syncs to Firebase in real-time. Access from multiple devices using your Shop Code. Requires internet for initial sync.
💾
Backup Your Data
Sidebar (☰) → Backup Data downloads a .json file. To restore, Settings → Restore Backup → select the file.
📊
Export Sales (CSV / Excel / PDF)
Sidebar (☰) → Export to Excel or Export PDF Report. Also available from the Reports tab.
⚠️Important: In Local Mode, clearing your browser's site data will erase all POSH data. Back up regularly or switch to Cloud Mode.
